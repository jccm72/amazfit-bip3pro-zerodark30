⚠️ Why did Zepp say "Unrecognized QR Code"?

Important: The QR scanner at the top of the Zepp App Home screen is for Pairing New Devices only!
To install custom watchfaces, you must use the Developer Mode Scanner:
  1. Open Zepp App → Profile → Settings → About
  2. Tap the Zepp logo 7 times until it says "Developer Mode enabled"
  3. Go to Profile → Amazfit Bip 3 Pro → Developer Mode
  4. Tap Scan (top right + icon) and scan the watchface QR code or select it from Photos
